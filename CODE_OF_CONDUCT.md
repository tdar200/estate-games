# Code of Conduct

Estate follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Please read it. It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you represent
the project.

## In short

We want this to be a friendly place to work on a game, for everyone, whatever their age, body,
background, identity, experience or ability.

Please:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different views and ways of working.
- Give and take feedback gracefully, about the work rather than the person.
- Own your mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the whole community.

Not okay:

- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting a problem

To report behaviour that breaks this code, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository: open
the **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say in the title that it is a
conduct report. Only the maintainers can see it. You can also use that channel to ask the
maintainers for another way to talk.

Maintainers will review every report, keep it as private as they can, and act fairly. That can mean
a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
